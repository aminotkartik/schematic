02 / SENSE MORE. WASTE LESS.

Presence is a signal.
Intelligence is the difference.

S3KM1110 · 24 GHzFMCW RADAR
SENSOR→TARGET→PRESENCE→ESP32

Even stillness has a signature.

24 GHz FMCW radar detects human presence and micro-motion within the configured sensing region.

↳ Radar reflections from static objects and environmental geometry require appropriate placement and calibration.

Motion ≠ continuous presence. PIR detects movement/activity rather than continuously proving that a person remains present.

01
SENSE

Read the room.

Three mmWave inputs and two PIR activity channels capture raw signals. They don’t make the decision.

02
INTERPRET

Confirm, then act.

The ESP32-S3 validates a stable input before the occupancy engine changes a zone’s state.

ESP32-S3 → OCCUPANCY ENGINE
03
TRACK

Give emptiness a little time.

Each zone keeps its own inactivity timer. Confirmed presence resets it; PIR activity restarts its grace period.

ZONE STATE → IDLE TIMER
04
WARN

A heads-up. Not a surprise.

At 10 simulated minutes, the zone turns amber. The OLED shows a countdown and the buzzer signals a warning.

10 MINUTES → BUZZER + OLED
05
CONTROL

Switch off. Welcome back.

At 15 minutes, lights and fans shut down in that zone only. Returning presence automatically restores power.
